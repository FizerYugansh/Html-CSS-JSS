Biography on Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo is a Portuguese professional footballer who plays as a forward for Serie A club Juventus and captains the Portugal national team.

He is widely regarded as one of the greatest footballers of all time. Ronaldo has won five Ballon d'Or awards, four European Golden Shoes, and has been named to the UEFA Team of the Year and FIFPro World XI multiple times.

Ronaldo began his senior club career playing for Sporting CP, before signing with Manchester United in 2003, where he won three Premier League titles, a FA Cup, two Football League Cups, and the UEFA Champions League. In 2009, he moved to Real Madrid for a then-world record transfer fee of £80 million, where he won numerous trophies including four Champions League titles.

In 2018, Ronaldo transferred to Juventus for a fee of €100 million, becoming the most expensive player in Italian football history. He has also had a successful international career, winning the UEFA European Championship in 2016 and the UEFA Nations League in 2019 with Portugal.

Off the field, Ronaldo is known for his philanthropy and has donated millions to various causes, including children's hospitals and disaster relief efforts. He is also a global ambassador for several brands and has a significant social media presence.
